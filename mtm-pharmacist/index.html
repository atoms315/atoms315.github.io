Why this work matters

The client is a national health company's Clinical Engagement Services Center. Pharmacists there run Medication Therapy Management, adherence, diabetes, polypharmacy, Star-ratings adherence and retrospective drug-utilization programs for plan members, all by phone, all from home.

A good review finds the duplicated therapy, the interaction the fourth prescriber never saw, the pill that stopped being refilled in March. You call the member, you call the provider, you write it down properly. That is the whole job, and it is a clinical one.

Telephonic, not a counterScheduled consultations and comprehensive medication reviews with members. No dispensing, no drive-through, no inventory.
Several programs, one platformMTM, adherence, diabetes, polypharmacy, STARS and retrospective DUR, worked inside an MTM platform alongside the usual Microsoft tools.
Measured on quality and volumeThere are productivity and quality targets. The program is behind on hiring and busy, so the pace is real.
01 — The role

Clinical Pharmacist on an MTM team. Fully remote. You report into the client's program leads; Equity Staffing Group is your employer of record.

You'd run the medication review end to end: the call, the findings, the recommendation, the record.

01
Consult with members by phone

Telephonic consultations and comprehensive medication reviews, scheduled through the program's platform.

02
Find what's wrong with the regimen

Review medications and clinical information for gaps in care, adherence problems, drug interactions, high-risk medications and therapeutic opportunities. Assess dosing, effectiveness and likely adverse reactions.

03
Educate, then recommend

Patient education on medications and disease-state management. Clinical recommendations go to the member and to their providers.

04
Document it accurately

Patient care, interventions and recommendations recorded in full, HIPAA kept, productivity and quality expectations met.

You need
  • An active, unrestricted pharmacist license in the state you live in. Any state except California.
  • Working knowledge of disease-state management and the 5 Core Elements of MTM.
  • 1+ year working remotely or independently in a cross-functional team.
  • Comfort running an MTM platform and several applications at once.
  • Clear phone communication, tidy documentation, good time management.
  • Intermediate Word, Outlook and Excel.
Helps, not required
  • PharmD.
  • 2+ years in retail, community or PBM pharmacy. New-graduate experiential rotations count.
  • Call-center experience.
  • A diabetes-related certification.
  • Bilingual in Cantonese, Korean, Mandarin, Russian, Spanish or Vietnamese.
  • Extra licenses in AL, ID, KS, KY, LA, MD, MN, MO, OK, OR, TN, VT or WV.
The plain caveats
  • It is a contract, 4–6 months to start, with the potential for extension or conversion to full-time.
  • Shifts rotate between 9 AM and 8 PM Central, Monday to Friday. Extended hours and occasional weekends can be required.
  • There are productivity and quality metrics, and the program is behind on hiring. Expect a full calendar.
  • Medical, dental and vision start after 60 days, not day one.
  • The program is open to pharmacists in every state except California at this time.

The posting asks for 2+ years of MTM experience. The program will take recent graduates who are licensed. If you are somewhere in between, reply anyway and let April make the call.
