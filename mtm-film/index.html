What you'd do

  • Telephonic consultations and comprehensive medication reviews with plan members.
  • Review medications and clinical information for gaps in care, adherence concerns, drug interactions, high-risk medications and therapeutic opportunities.
  • Assess regimens for effectiveness, dosing and potential adverse reactions.
  • Educate members on medications and disease-state management; send recommendations to members and providers.
  • Document care, interventions and recommendations accurately, keep HIPAA, and meet productivity and quality expectations.

Who fits

  • Active, unrestricted pharmacist license in your state of residence (any state except California).
  • Strong knowledge of disease-state management and the 5 Core Elements of MTM.
  • 1+ year working remotely or independently in a cross-functional environment.
  • Able to navigate an MTM platform and multiple applications at once; intermediate Word, Outlook and Excel.
  • Helps: PharmD; 2+ years retail, community or PBM pharmacy; call-center experience; a diabetes certification; Cantonese, Korean, Mandarin, Russian, Spanish or Vietnamese; extra licenses in AL, ID, KS, KY, LA, MD, MN, MO, OK, OR, TN, VT or WV.

The posting asks for 2+ years of MTM experience; recent graduates who are licensed will be considered.

How the process runs

  1. 2-minute pre-screen form. You'll land on April's calendar right after.
  2. 15-minute call with April. Email your resume to atoms@equitysg.com before it if you haven't already.
  3. Submittal to the program, same day.
  4. Phone or video interview with the program's clinical team, then the offer and onboarding from Equity.
