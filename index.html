Remote pharmacist hiring campaign

Oct 2026 · staffing contract

This is a high-volume search for 200 remote clinical pharmacists across two training classes. I built every candidate-facing piece and the back end behind them: a narrated film, a candidate pack microsite, a pre-screen form, and n8n workflows that score, log, follow up and report.

Candidate experienceFilm and candidate pack

A 90-second narrated film and a microsite that put pay, schedule, caveats and the process up front, so the 15-minute call is spent on fit.

Intake2-minute pre-screen

Mobile-friendly n8n form with an optional resume. Each submission emails me a fit score, updates the contact table and sends the candidate to Calendly.

Follow-upConsent-gated SMS

Registered Twilio campaign. Inbound replies are classified as yes, other or opt-out. Follow-ups go only to contacts with a consent record.

ReportingFriday pipeline email

Every Friday at 3 PM Arizona time I get the week's texts, replies, form submissions, opt-outs and pipeline totals by state.

Talent intelligenceRanked market map

Pharmacists scored and sorted by tier and outreach wave, then a bulk enrichment test on the top 50 to measure email match rates before scaling to the top 1,000.

Recruiter Launch Kit

2026 · demoed at AI Recruiting Summit and RecFest

Paste an intake call transcript into a form. Eleven n8n steps later, the recruiter has a role folder, a launch kit document, a follow-up reminder on the calendar and a link in email and Slack. The kit has seven sections: role brief, screening guide, sourcing plan, hiring manager alignment, follow-up flags, a launch note and reminders.

  1. Intake form: role + transcript
  2. Create role folder in Drive
  3. Create transcript doc
  4. Save raw transcript
  5. Claude drafts the launch kit
  6. Clean the text
  7. Create launch kit doc
  8. Insert the kit
  9. Reminder on the calendar in 2 days
  10. Email the links
  11. Slack the links
From the prompt

Do not pretend the transcript answered things it did not answer. Separate confirmed intake details from AI-inferred recruiter recommendations. If something is missing, vague, conflicting, or risky, put it under Recruiter Follow-Up Flags.
